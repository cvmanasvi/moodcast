How we read your mood

🧠 Text detection

When you describe your mood in a sentence, a classifier trained on labeled examples predicts one of 6 moods with a confidence score — no keyword guesswork.

🎵 Audio matching

Tracks are ranked by cosine similarity between their audio features (valence, energy, tempo, and more) and the learned centroid for your target mood.

🎬 Plot-aware movies

Movie mood probabilities come from a classifier reading plot and genre text, blended with rating and popularity for the final ranking.
